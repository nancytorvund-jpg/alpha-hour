# Alpha Hours

A premium after-school program that runs the Alpha academic model on Timeback inside existing Alpha School buildings, for families who do not attend Alpha. It is designed to make money, use real estate Alpha already pays for, and feed full-time Alpha enrollment.

**Owner:** Nancy Wisniewski Torvund, proposed Head of Alpha Hours.

## The model in one paragraph

The full Alpha academic day, after school. Both subjects (Math and Reading/Writing), mixed K-8, four hours a week on Timeback. Each family picks a two-night track, Monday/Wednesday or Tuesday/Thursday from 5:00 to 7:00 pm, or a single four-hour Saturday block, morning or afternoon. Four cohorts share every room, so a campus enrolls four times its seat count. Families enroll one seven-week session at a time; there are five sessions a year. Priced above every competitor at $4,500 per session in premium markets, about a third of full Alpha tuition.

## Headline numbers

- **East Coast premium pilot (Fall 2026)** at full capacity: 1,720 enrolled, ~$38.7M revenue, ~$32.7M net, ~$40M-$60M feeder pipeline.
- **National rollout** opens January 2027.
- **Full 40-campus network at capacity:** ~$246M revenue, ~$210M net, ~$225M-$340M feeder pipeline, which is about 0.6% of a ~$39B serviceable market. No ceiling.
