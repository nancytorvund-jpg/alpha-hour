The idea

Run the complete Alpha day, mastery academics on Timeback plus a life skills workshop, after school and on Saturdays, for families who do not attend Alpha. A family picks a two-night track, Monday/Wednesday or Tuesday/Thursday, 5:00 to 7:00 pm, or a single four-hour Saturday block, inside Alpha campuses that sit empty after the school day and on weekends. Either way the child gets 100 minutes of focused mastery academics on Timeback and 80 minutes of life skills a week, both tracks identical, both subjects always included, supported by guides hired and trained just for this program. Families enroll one session at a time. Start with grades K-8 and scale across the network.

How it runs

●The whole Alpha day, every sitting

Each sitting is the whole Alpha day: a Limitless launch, 25-minute mastery sessions on Timeback (both subjects), a life skills workshop, and shoutouts. No single-subject option.

●Two tracks, two Saturday blocks, four cohorts

Every campus runs four cohorts a week: Monday/Wednesday, Tuesday/Thursday, Saturday morning, and Saturday afternoon. The same room and the same guide team serve all four, and every cohort gets the same four weekly hours. Enrolled students per campus is four times the room capacity: two weekday tracks plus two Saturday blocks. A 100-seat room fills to 400 enrolled students.

●Five sessions, sign up one at a time

Each session runs about seven weeks. A one-session minimum lowers the barrier to trying Alpha, which widens the funnel and cycles more families through every seat.

●The trial sells the school

A child doing Alpha every week, mastery data climbing, is the highest-intent lead Alpha can have. One conversion is worth $160k to $325k.

Why these campuses  ·  VIP markets, proven demand

The pilot is the East Coast VIP corridor: New York, Boston, Greenwich, Palm Beach, and Miami Beach. It is chosen on data, not hope.

●Demand is already evidenced

Alpha runs its ultra-high-net-worth admissions events in exactly these five markets. The inquiry and interest data from that program show concentrated demand in each city.

●Highest-value campuses in the network

Every pilot market except Palm Beach carries full Alpha tuition of $60k to $75k. Premium pricing holds, and every conversion is worth $300k or more in lifetime tuition. Start the feeder where value is highest.

●Dense wealth that talks

Among the highest concentrations of UHNW families in the country, in tight social networks where the program spreads by referral. Filling one cohort seeds the next.

●Nancy's base and a tight corridor

She moves into this role from admissions leadership at Alpha New York, bringing relationships across the corridor. The Northeast sites are close enough for one coordinator to cover several.
