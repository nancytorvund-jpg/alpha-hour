# Alpha Hours Redesign: One Block Per Evening, Saturday Cohorts, New Pricing

**Date:** July 9, 2026
**Status:** Approved by Nancy, ready for implementation planning
**Scope:** Rewrite all 13 repo documents to the new program design. No new documents except this spec.

## Decisions (all confirmed by Nancy)

1. **One 2-hour block per weekday evening** replaces the two 1-hour blocks.
2. **Saturday added** with two 4-hour blocks (AM and PM), each delivering the same weekly hours as a weekday track.
3. **Room multiplier stays x4:** Mon/Wed track, Tue/Thu track, Saturday AM, Saturday PM. All enrollment counts in the current docs are unchanged.
4. **Pricing rises to $4,500 premium / $3,500 standard** per 7-week session.
5. **Name changes to "Alpha Hours"** (plural). Repo slug stays `alpha-hour`.

## The new program model

Every cohort gets 4 hours a week on Timeback, 28 hours per session. This is the full Alpha academic dose: Alpha's own students run about 2 hours of core academics a day, and Alpha's 2x claim is based on that dose. Both subjects every sitting, roughly half Math and half Reading/Writing.

### Weekday schedule (Mon through Thu)

| | Time |
|---|---|
| Setup | 4:45 to 5:00 pm |
| Block | 5:00 to 7:00 pm |
| Dismissal and reset | 7:00 to 7:15 pm |

The 6:00 to 6:15 transition window no longer exists. Families pick the Mon/Wed or Tue/Thu track as before.

### Saturday schedule

| | Time |
|---|---|
| Setup | 8:45 to 9:00 am |
| AM block | 9:00 am to 1:00 pm |
| Transition | 1:00 to 2:00 pm (staggered pickup and drop-off) |
| PM block | 2:00 to 6:00 pm |
| Reset | 6:00 to 6:15 pm |

No lunch is served; the AM cohort leaves before the PM cohort arrives. Snacks only, preserving the no-meals licensing posture. Each 4-hour block includes a short supervised break and is structured as two 2-hour instructional periods.

### Cohorts and capacity

Four cohorts per room per week: Mon/Wed, Tue/Thu, Saturday AM, Saturday PM. Enrolled per campus = room capacity x 4 (two weekday tracks plus two Saturday blocks). All published capacity figures stay: pilot 1,720, national 10,920, per-campus rows unchanged.

### Sessions

Unchanged: five sessions a year, about seven weeks each, one-session minimum, both subjects always included.

## Pricing

| Tier | Per session | Full year (5 sessions) |
|---|---|---|
| Standard markets | $3,500 | $17,500 |
| Premium markets | $4,500 | $22,500 |

- Full year premium = $22,500, about 35% of a $65k Alpha tuition. The "about a third of Alpha" frame survives.
- Per-hour: $161 (28 hours per session). Copy changes from "inside the elite 1:1 range" to "just under elite 1:1 tutors, who cover one subject."
- Pilot prices every campus at premium tier, as before.

## Staffing changes

- Guide staff week: four ~2.5-hour evenings (10 h) plus a ~10-hour Saturday = about 20 hours, up from ~11.
- Ratios unchanged: ~1 guide per 10 students plus a lead, two-adult minimum, closer to 1:8 for K-2-heavy sites.
- Hourly rates unchanged ($22 to $45 by metro; leads $30 to $55). The blended day-plus-evening role stays at $50,000 to $85,000; the Insight 3 line changes from "an 11-hour evening-only job" to "a 20-hour evenings-and-Saturday job."
- Facility fee rises from ~$35,000 to ~$45,000 per site per year (five operating days instead of four).
