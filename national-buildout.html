TAM: about 4.4M US K-8 students in households that can afford premium academics (roughly the top 12% by income), at $22,500 a year. SAM: the ~1.75M of those in the metros where Alpha operates. Today's entire network at full capacity is about 0.6% of the serviceable market. The bars are not to scale; if they were, today's slice would be a hairline. Alpha adds roughly 20 campuses a year, so the number climbs toward the market, not toward a cap.

Today's network at full capacity is a rounding error against the market. The constraint is buildings, not demand, and Alpha is opening them across the country. Alpha Hours grows with every one.

Capacities are room capacity per block (enrolled is four cohorts, room capacity x 4, from two weekday tracks and two Saturday blocks): Austin 300, Chicago 300, Miami 150, East Coast pilot campuses as set, all others a conservative 50. Premium pricing of $4,500 per session ($22,500 per year) at every campus. Roster is Alpha's 41 current campuses from alpha.school, less Alpha High Austin (grades 9-12). Market sizing is an estimate at stated income assumptions; a stricter filter still leaves a TAM in the tens of billions. Feeder pipeline is future Alpha tuition created, not booked revenue.
